This Lean formalization was developed with assistance from OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex. ChatGPT was used for mathematical discussions, proof planning, and reviewing the formalization, while Codex was used to implement and revise the Lean proofs.

Victor Gabillon coordinated this work and maintains the repository.

ChatGPT and Codex are tools, not human authors or independent sources of mathematical authority. Victor Gabillon confirmed that he was the only coauthor to check the AI-assisted revisions. On 2026-10-17 he approved the exact manuscript correction: “Victor Gabillon reviewed the resulting AI-assisted changes.” That sentence is now included, preserving the surrounding assistance disclosure and making no collective review/responsibility claim. It does not assign sole authorship of the mathematics or copyright of all contributions to the hosting account. Any further named contribution or rights-holder attribution needs confirmation from the relevant people. Lean and Mathlib retain their own contributor credit and licenses.
