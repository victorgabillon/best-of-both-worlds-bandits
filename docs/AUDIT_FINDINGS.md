## Declaration-count correction and safety scope

The Step 22 exhaustive private-part inspection corrected the earlier **1,740** count to **1,742** project declarations, with **1,425** theorem constants. Two older private `AdaptiveBanditProcess.trajectoryLaw_isProbabilityMeasure` helpers became visible when every module's private part was imported. No mathematical source changed. Historical reports retain their original counts; they are not silently rewritten.

The portable inspector imports every mathematical module's private part and checks this complete inventory. The allowed transitive axioms are exactly the expected foundational set `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`; an unexpected axiom causes failure. The principal inspector explicitly prints axioms for 19 endpoint/variant declarations.

Seven of the 1,742 names are Lean-generated partial nonproof runtime helpers ending in `._unsafe_rec`. They are explicitly distinguished from the **1,735 safe nonpartial constants**. The safety check inspects safe project types and values for unsafe/partial dependencies; a zero result is required. Lean's own module replay excludes unsafe/partial runtime declarations and checks the safe mathematical declarations, including private proofs. Imported libraries remain trusted. The report must therefore say “1,742 declarations inventoried and axiom-inspected; 57 modules kernel-replayed,” not “1,742 individual constants kernel-replayed.”
